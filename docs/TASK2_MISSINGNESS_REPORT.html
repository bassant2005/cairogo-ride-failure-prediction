1. Executive Summary & Objective

In Phase 1, Task 2 investigates missing values in weather (from trips.csv) and rating_avg (from drivers.csv joined to trips.csv).

The objective is to determine whether missingness is MCAR (Missing Completely At Random) or MNAR (Missing Not At Random / Informative):

  • If random, missing values could be replaced by a simple mean or dropped.
  • If informative, the fact that data is missing is a predictive risk signal that must be preserved.
Key Finding
Both features exhibit statistically significant, informative missingness (MNAR):
  • Trips with missing weather have a +4.06% higher failure rate (χ² = 13.47, p = 0.00024).
  • Trips with unrated drivers have a +5.04% higher failure rate (χ² = 47.60, p = 5.22 × 10⁻¹²).
